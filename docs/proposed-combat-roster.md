# Proposed Nobel Combat Roster

This document proposes additional playable fighters for **Nobel Combat**, a 3D fighting game in which Nobel laureates use
exaggerated attacks inspired by their prize-winning research.

**Modeling reference disclaimer:** Do not trust the character descriptions below as authoritative visual references. Before
modeling a character, do a web image search covering multiple periods of the laureate's career. Choose a recognizable
appearance from their active scientific career, rather than automatically using their Nobel-ceremony or late-life
appearance. Preserve distinctive features such as hairstyle, facial hair, glasses, build, and typical clothing.

## Character design principles

Characters should be recognizable as the scientists, but they are not intended to be photorealistic reproductions. Each fighter should have a distinctive silhouette, clothing, hairstyle, and combat style.

Do not automatically model laureates at the age when they received the Nobel Prize. Prefer a recognizable adult appearance from the period when they were actively conducting the work associated with the character. This should produce a roster with a reasonable range of apparent ages.

Published heights are unavailable for most laureates. Therefore, **modeling height is an approximate character-design target rather than a biographical claim**. Relative height and body type are primarily intended to keep the roster visually varied.

Special attacks should be ridiculous but scientifically recognizable. Ideally, a player who knows nothing about the scientist should learn something about the Nobel-winning research simply by seeing the attacks.

# Structural Biology and Biochemistry

## David Baker

**Nobel Prize:** Chemistry, 2024, computational protein design. Baker's work demonstrated that entirely new proteins can be computationally designed rather than merely discovered in nature. ([Nobel Prize](https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/chemistry/2024/press-release/b/?utm_source=chatgpt.com))

**Modeling reference:** Baker in middle age. Approximately 5'10" modeling height. Medium build. Short brown or graying hair, somewhat casual appearance. Glasses optional depending on reference period. Casual academic clothing, typically an open-collar shirt, sweater, or simple jacket rather than formal attire.

**Combat style:** Creates new molecular machines during combat.

### Special attacks

1. **Rosetta Fold**
   A loose amino-acid chain erupts from Baker's hands and rapidly folds into a rigid protein weapon.

2. **De Novo Design**
   Baker specifies a function and constructs a completely novel protein to perform it. The resulting protein changes depending on combat context, such as a shield, projectile, cage, or melee weapon.

3. **Top7**
   Baker summons the famous designed Top7 protein as an enormous molecular construct. It assembles around the opponent and traps them inside a structure that never previously existed in nature.

## Thomas Steitz

**Nobel Prize:** Chemistry, 2009, studies of the structure and function of the ribosome.

**Modeling reference:** Steitz in middle age, during his active structural-biology career. Medium build. Distinctive straight white hair, somewhat long over the ears, with a characteristic white chin-strap beard and mustache area largely clean-shaven. Wire-frame glasses. Usually dressed as a conventional academic: collared shirt, sweater or jacket, occasionally a tie. The white hair and chin-strap beard should be treated as defining visual features of the character.

**Combat style:** Uses the ribosome as a gigantic molecular weapons factory.

### Special attacks

1. **50S Slam**
   The large ribosomal subunit materializes above the opponent and crashes downward.

2. **Peptidyl Transferase**
   The ribosome rapidly polymerizes a peptide chain that extends outward as a molecular whip.

3. **Translation Termination**
   A complete ribosome encloses the opponent. Translation races forward until a stop codon appears, violently terminating the attack and ejecting the opponent.

## Joachim Frank

**Nobel Prize:** Chemistry, 2017, development of cryo-electron microscopy for high-resolution structure determination of biomolecules. Frank developed methods for combining noisy two-dimensional electron-microscope images into sharp three-dimensional structures. ([Nobel Prize](https://www.nobelprize.org/laureate/945?utm_source=chatgpt.com))

**Modeling reference:** Frank around the 1990s to early 2000s. Approximately 5'9" modeling height. Slim to average build. Graying hair, glasses, academic clothing such as a button-down shirt and jacket.

**Combat style:** Freezes, images, averages, and reconstructs opponents.

### Special attacks

1. **Vitrification**
   The opponent is instantly trapped in a thin layer of vitreous ice.

2. **Particle Average**
   Hundreds of noisy images of Frank surround the opponent, align, and average into a much stronger Frank.

3. **3D Reconstruction**
   Two-dimensional projections circle the opponent and combine into a gigantic three-dimensional molecular reconstruction that attacks the original.

## Dorothy Crowfoot Hodgkin

**Nobel Prize:** Chemistry, 1964, X-ray determination of the structures of important biochemical substances, including vitamin B12. ([Nobel Prize](https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/chemistry/1964/hodgkin/?utm_source=chatgpt.com))

**Modeling reference:** Hodgkin in her 40s or early 50s. Approximately 5'5" modeling height. Slim build. Dark, wavy hair with some gray. Dresses, skirts, cardigans, and practical mid-century academic clothing.

**Combat style:** Crystals, diffraction, X-rays, and giant molecular structures.

### Special attacks

1. **X-Ray Diffraction**
   Fires intersecting X-ray beams through a crystal, producing a spreading diffraction-pattern attack.

2. **Electron Density**
   A glowing electron-density map forms around the opponent and progressively constrains their movement.

3. **Vitamin B12**
   A gigantic, elaborate B12 molecular structure crystallizes above the arena and crashes onto the opponent.

## Christian B. Anfinsen

**Nobel Prize:** Chemistry, 1972, work establishing the relationship between amino-acid sequence and the biologically active conformation of proteins. Baker's Nobel materials explicitly identify Anfinsen's work as foundational to modern protein design. ([Nobel Prize](https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/chemistry/2024/baker/471011-david-baker-podcast/?utm_source=chatgpt.com))

**Modeling reference:** Anfinsen in middle age. Approximately 5'10" modeling height. Average build. Short dark hair becoming gray. Glasses. Shirt, tie, and laboratory coat or conservative academic jacket.

**Combat style:** Unfolding and spontaneous refolding.

### Special attacks

1. **Denaturation**
   Unfolds the opponent into a floppy, weakened form.

2. **Sequence Determines Structure**
   An amino-acid sequence appears in midair and rapidly folds into a weapon.

3. **Anfinsen's Dogma**
   Anfinsen completely unfolds, apparently disappearing into a random chain, then spontaneously refolds behind the opponent at full strength.

# Genetics and Molecular Biology

## Jennifer Doudna

**Nobel Prize:** Chemistry, 2020, development of CRISPR-Cas9 genome editing. ([Nobel Prize](https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/list/all-nobel-prizes-in-chemistry/?utm_source=chatgpt.com))

**Modeling reference:** Doudna in her 40s to early 50s. Approximately 5'8" modeling height. Slim to average build. Shoulder-length blonde hair. Professional but relatively informal academic clothing.

**Combat style:** Precise targeted genome editing.

### Special attacks

1. **Guide RNA**
   A glowing RNA strand searches the arena and locks onto a specific target on the opponent.

2. **Cas9 Cleavage**
   A huge Cas9 molecular complex follows the guide RNA and cuts the selected target.

3. **CRISPR Rewrite**
   Doudna edits the opponent's genome in real time, temporarily transforming their anatomy and disabling selected abilities.

## Barbara McClintock

**Nobel Prize:** Physiology or Medicine, 1983, discovery of mobile genetic elements.

**Modeling reference:** McClintock in her 40s to 50s during her Cold Spring Harbor years. Approximately 5'5" modeling height. Slender build. Short, practical dark hair becoming gray. Plain shirt, trousers, field jacket, or laboratory clothing rather than formal Nobel-era clothing.

**Combat style:** Extremely mobile fighter based on maize genetics and transposable elements.

### Special attacks

1. **Ac/Ds**
   Activator and Dissociation elements leap around the arena, disrupting anything they insert into.

2. **Maize Chromosome**
   Giant brightly colored maize chromosomes emerge from the ground and strike the opponent.

3. **TRANSPOSON**
   McClintock disappears from one location and inserts herself somewhere else in the arena, usually directly behind the opponent. Her original position is left with a visible chromosomal gap.

## Elizabeth Blackburn

**Nobel Prize:** Physiology or Medicine, 2009, discovery of how chromosomes are protected by telomeres and telomerase.

**Modeling reference:** Blackburn in her 40s to 50s. Approximately 5'6" modeling height. Average build. Short to medium blonde hair. Glasses in some periods. Professional academic clothing.

**Combat style:** Manipulates chromosome ends and cellular lifespan.

### Special attacks

1. **Telomere Cap**
   Chromosome ends form a protective shield around Blackburn.

2. **Telomerase**
   Rebuilds Blackburn's damaged telomeres and restores a portion of health.

3. **Replicative Senescence**
   The opponent's telomeres visibly shorten with each rapid cell division until the opponent becomes weak, gray, and unable to continue dividing.

## Katalin Kariko

**Nobel Prize:** Physiology or Medicine, 2023, discoveries concerning nucleoside base modifications that enabled effective mRNA vaccines.

**Modeling reference:** Kariko in her 40s to 50s. Approximately 5'5" modeling height. Slim build. Short blonde hair. Laboratory coat or simple professional academic clothing.

**Combat style:** Delivers modified mRNA that temporarily changes cellular protein production.

### Special attacks

1. **Modified Nucleoside**
   Substitutes modified nucleosides into an incoming RNA attack, allowing it to evade the opponent's defenses.

2. **Lipid Nanoparticle**
   Launches spherical delivery particles containing mRNA payloads.

3. **mRNA Translation**
   Delivered mRNA hijacks the opponent's ribosomes, forcing their cells to manufacture Kariko's selected combat protein.

## Sidney Altman

**Nobel Prize:** Chemistry, 1989, catalytic properties of RNA.

**Modeling reference:** Altman in his 40s to 50s. Approximately 5'9" modeling height. Average build. Dark curly hair becoming gray. Glasses. Shirt and tie, sweater, or laboratory coat.

**Combat style:** RNA that refuses to behave like passive genetic information.

### Special attacks

1. **RNase P**
   Catalytic RNA slices through an incoming molecular attack.

2. **RNA Catalyst**
   RNA strands begin independently catalyzing reactions throughout the arena.

3. **RNA Is the Enzyme**
   Altman removes every protein enzyme from the arena. The opponent assumes the attack has failed until an enormous catalytic RNA folds and strikes.

## Thomas Cech

**Nobel Prize:** Chemistry, 1989, catalytic properties of RNA.

**Modeling reference:** Cech in his 40s. Approximately 5'11" modeling height. Lean build. Brown hair, glasses, relatively youthful academic appearance. Button-down shirt with rolled sleeves or laboratory coat.

**Combat style:** Self-splicing RNA and ribozymes.

### Special attacks

1. **Group I Intron**
   A giant RNA intron wraps around the opponent.

2. **Self-Splice**
   Cech cuts himself out of an incoming attack and reconnects on the opposite side.

3. **Ribozyme**
   A huge folded RNA molecule catalyzes its own attack without any protein assistance.

## David Baltimore

**Nobel Prize:** Physiology or Medicine, 1975, discoveries concerning the interaction between tumour viruses and the genetic material of the cell. ([Nobel Prize](https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/medicine/1975/baltimore/facts/))

**Modeling reference:** Use the 1975 portrait period: dark hair swept back with prominent sideburns, rectangular metal-frame glasses, a full dark beard and mustache, dark jacket, light shirt, and patterned tie. The reference is an upper-torso portrait, so height and build are unspecified.

**Combat style:** Retroviral information flow.

### Special attacks

1. **Reverse Transcriptase**
   An RNA attack suddenly reverses direction and becomes DNA.

2. **Proviral Integration**
   Viral DNA inserts into the opponent's genome and remains dormant for several seconds.

3. **Central Dogma Reversed**
   The arena's normal molecular information flow reverses from RNA back into DNA, unleashing a retroviral transformation attack.

# Cell Biology and Neuroscience

## Rita Levi-Montalcini

**Nobel Prize:** Physiology or Medicine, 1986, discovery of growth factors, particularly nerve growth factor. ([Nobel Prize](https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/medicine/1986/press-release/1000/?utm_source=chatgpt.com))

**Modeling reference:** Levi-Montalcini in her 50s to 60s. Approximately 5'3" modeling height. Very slender build. Carefully styled dark hair becoming gray. Elegant, highly distinctive tailored clothing and jewelry. Her consistently well-groomed and elegant appearance was specifically noted by Washington University. ([Department of Neuroscience](https://neuroscience.wustl.edu/items/rita-levi-montalcini/?utm_source=chatgpt.com))

**Combat style:** Controls explosive neuronal growth.

### Special attacks

1. **NGF**
   Neurites rapidly sprout from the ground and pursue the opponent.

2. **Axon Guidance**
   Growing axons track the opponent around obstacles and across the arena.

3. **Growth Factor**
   An enormous network of neurons erupts across the entire stage, branching repeatedly until the opponent is completely entangled.

## Linda Buck

**Nobel Prize:** Physiology or Medicine, 2004, odorant receptors and organization of the olfactory system. Buck and Richard Axel showed how a large family of odorant receptors produces combinatorial representations of smells. ([Fred Hutch](https://www.fredhutch.org/en/people/b/linda-buck.html?utm_source=chatgpt.com))

**Modeling reference:** Buck in her late 40s to 50s. Approximately 5'6" modeling height. Slim build. Shoulder-length brown hair. Understated laboratory or academic clothing.

**Combat style:** Smell. This should be one of the more overtly comedic characters.

### Special attacks

1. **Odorant Receptor**
   Buck selects a receptor combination that identifies and tracks the opponent regardless of visibility.

2. **Combinatorial Code**
   Multiple individually weak odors combine into a powerful sensory attack.

3. **OLFACTORY OVERLOAD**
   Every odorant receptor fires simultaneously. The opponent is engulfed by a visible cloud representing an indescribably horrible smell and collapses while trying to escape.

# Chemical Biology

## Carolyn Bertozzi

**Nobel Prize:** Chemistry, 2022, click chemistry and bioorthogonal chemistry. ([Nobel Prize](https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/list/all-nobel-prizes-in-chemistry/?utm_source=chatgpt.com))

**Modeling reference:** Bertozzi in her 40s to 50s. Approximately 5'7" modeling height. Athletic to average build. Short brown hair, often casually styled. Casual academic clothing. A younger alternate skin could reference her bass-guitar-playing period, which is documented in Nobel biographical material. ([Nobel Prize](https://www.nobelprize.org/stories/women-who-changed-science/carolyn-bertozzi/?utm_source=chatgpt.com))

**Combat style:** Performs chemistry directly on living opponents without disturbing their normal biology.

### Special attacks

1. **Bioorthogonal Reaction**
   A chemical attack passes harmlessly through everything except its specifically tagged target.

2. **Click**
   Two molecular components instantly snap together around the opponent.

3. **Glycocalyx**
   A massive fluorescent sugar coat appears over the opponent, followed by click-reactive molecules that attach throughout the surface.

## K. Barry Sharpless

**Nobel Prizes:** Chemistry, 2001 and 2022. His first recognized chirally catalyzed oxidation reactions. His second recognized click chemistry. ([Nobel Prize](https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/chemistry/2001/sharpless/facts/?utm_source=chatgpt.com))

**Modeling reference:** Sharpless in his 50s to early 60s. Approximately 5'10" modeling height. Average build. Receding gray hair. Glasses. Casual laboratory clothing.

**Combat style:** Fast, reliable chemical reactions with almost no wasted motion.

### Special attacks

1. **Sharpless Epoxidation**
   A stereoselective oxidation strike generates a three-membered epoxide ring around the target.

2. **Click**
   Two harmless projectiles attach to opposite sides of the opponent and then snap together.

3. **Second Nobel**
   Sharpless performs an oxidation combo, pauses as though the attack is finished, then unexpectedly receives a second full special meter and launches a click-chemistry finisher.

# Fluorescence and Imaging

## Roger Tsien

**Nobel Prize:** Chemistry, 2008, discovery and development of green fluorescent protein. Tsien developed GFP variants with improved fluorescence and a palette of different colors. ([National Inventors Hall of Fame](https://www.invent.org/inductees/roger-tsien?utm_source=chatgpt.com))

**Modeling reference:** Use 2008 as the primary period and 2012 as corroboration. Short salt-and-pepper hair with gray temples, a high forehead, thin oval wire-frame glasses, and a clean-shaven narrow oval face. The 2008 reference supports a dark jacket, light small-check shirt, and narrow dark tie. No full-body estimate is available.

**Combat style:** Fluorescence, calcium signaling, and color.

### Special attacks

1. **GFP**
   The opponent begins glowing intense fluorescent green, making hiding impossible.

2. **Calcium Indicator**
   Tsien detects the opponent's intracellular calcium changes and anticipates their next movement.

3. **Fluorescent Palette**
   Successive GFP-derived proteins illuminate the entire arena in different wavelengths before combining into a blinding multicolor burst.

# Physics and Energy

## John B. Goodenough

**Nobel Prize:** Chemistry, 2019, development of lithium-ion batteries. Goodenough developed a cobalt-oxide cathode that enabled substantially higher-voltage lithium batteries. ([Nobel Prize](https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/chemistry/2019/goodenough/facts/?utm_source=chatgpt.com))

**Modeling reference:** Use the January 1964 MIT Lincoln Laboratory group photograph. Its evidence supports short dark neatly parted hair, a dark suit, white shirt, and dark tie. The group image does not reliably establish facial detail, glasses, height, or build.

**Combat style:** Stores energy and releases it at high voltage.

### Special attacks

1. **Lithium Ion**
   Lithium ions stream between electrodes, accumulating charge as Goodenough blocks attacks.

2. **Cobalt Oxide Cathode**
   Deploys a crystalline cathode shield that absorbs lithium ions and increases voltage.

3. **FULLY CHARGED**
   All damage accumulated during the round is converted into stored electrochemical energy and discharged through the opponent at once.

**Nobel Prize:** Physics, 2018, chirped pulse amplification.

**Modeling reference:** Use the 1985 doctoral-research period associated with the original CPA work: short dark hair, large dark rectangular glasses, and practical laboratory knitwear. The reference does not support curly hair, height, or build.

**Combat style:** Laser pulses that are stretched, amplified, and compressed.

   Stretches a laser pulse across the arena, making it temporarily weak but difficult to avoid.

2. **Amplify**

3. **Pulse Compression**
   The entire stretched pulse suddenly compresses into an ultrashort, extremely intense blast.

## John Bardeen

**Nobel Prizes:** Physics, 1956 and 1972, for the transistor and the theory of superconductivity.

**Modeling reference:** Use the 1956 period: receding dark hair, round glasses, a dark or neutral suit, white shirt, and striped tie. The period portrait does not establish height or build; it does not support unusually thick glasses or a blue suit.

**Combat style:** Two completely different Nobel-powered modes: semiconductor electronics and superconductivity.

### Special attacks

1. **Transistor**
   A tiny input attack controls an enormous amplified output strike.

2. **Cooper Pair**
   Electrons pair and sweep across the arena without electrical resistance.

3. **BCS Superconductivity**
   The arena drops toward absolute zero. Electrical resistance vanishes, Bardeen becomes surrounded by paired electrons, and a massive persistent current repeatedly strikes the opponent.

# Initial roster priorities

The strongest candidates are not necessarily the most famous scientists. Priority should go to characters whose research produces a **visually distinctive combat mechanic**.

The roster should avoid having every molecular biologist simply fire differently colored molecular projectiles. Each character should instead have a recognizable verb:

**Baker designs. Steitz translates. Frank reconstructs. Hodgkin diffracts. Doudna edits. Bertozzi clicks. McClintock transposes. Levi-Montalcini grows. Blackburn extends. Kariko delivers. Tsien fluoresces. Goodenough charges. Strickland amplifies. Sharpless reacts. Buck smells. Bardeen conducts. Altman catalyzes. Cech splices. Baltimore reverses. Anfinsen folds. Herzberg excites. Gabor reconstructs wavefronts.**

That distinction should drive animation, VFX, AI behavior, and fighting style as much as the individual attacks.
