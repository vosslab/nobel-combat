# Marie Curie source dossier

## Scope

This dossier records the research inspiration for Curie's three meter-selected specials. The shared
`SpecialDef` data owns their timing and balance. Every combat outcome is fictional game presentation:
it does not model radiation exposure, a medical effect, or deliberate harm with research tools.

## Source basis

- Curie's 1911 Nobel lecture describes repeated chemical separation followed by measurement of the
  products' activity. [Marie Curie, "Radium and the New Concepts in Chemistry" (1911)](https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/chemistry/1911/marie-curie/lecture/)
- The Nobel history records that Marie carried out chemical separations while Pierre measured
  results after successive steps. [Marie and Pierre Curie and the discovery of polonium and radium](https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/themes/marie-and-pierre-curie-and-the-discovery-of-polonium-and-radium/)
- The 1911 Chemistry prize recognized the discovery of polonium and radium, isolation of radium,
  and study of its nature and compounds. [Nobel Prize summary](https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/chemistry/1911/summary/)

## Research-motif specials

Tap the shared Special input with 100, 200, or 300 meter to release tiers one, two, or three.

- **Separation Step.** Real work: separation and activity measurements guided radium-salt
  concentration. [Curie lecture (1911)](https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/chemistry/1911/marie-curie/lecture/)
  Game fiction: a short-range strike resolves from the generic special scheduler.
- **Radium Glow.** Real work: the 1911 prize recognized radium's isolation and study of its
  compounds. [Nobel Prize summary](https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/chemistry/1911/summary/)
  Game fiction: a temporary slowing zone is an abstract arena signal, not a radiation simulation.
- **Polonium Burst.** Real work: Curie and her collaborators identified polonium while studying
  pitchblende. [Nobel history](https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/themes/marie-and-pierre-curie-and-the-discovery-of-polonium-and-radium/)
  Game fiction: a ring-pattern projectile burst is an abstract visual joke.

## Evidence limits

The sources establish Curie's scientific work. They do not establish combat techniques, damage,
timing, or visual effects; those remain authored game data.
