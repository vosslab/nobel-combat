# Structural biology and biochemistry roster dossier

This dossier records source checks for the five proposed structural-biology and biochemistry
fighters. Gameplay effects and captions are deliberately fictional; the linked scientific hook is
the factual inspiration. Accessed 2026-09-24.

## David Baker

- **Prize check:** Nobel Prize in Chemistry 2024, one half, "for computational protein design."
  See the [Nobel Prize facts page](https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/chemistry/2024/baker/facts/).
- **Proposed concept:** computational protein designer who builds molecular machines in the arena.
- **Visual reference set:** [UCLA's 2018 distinguished-lecture gallery](https://www.chemistry.ucla.edu/news/winter-2018-distinguished-lecture-prof-david-baker/)
  supplies an active-career lecture period; [Wikimedia Commons' 2024 Nobel portrait](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:David_Baker,_2024_Nobel_Prize_Laureate_in_Chemistry.jpg)
  supplies the later Nobel period. Both were downloaded to the private review scratch directory.
- **Image-review trace:** selected period is the active-presenter look, represented by the 2024
  Nobel portrait. Observed: loose wavy gray hair, rectangular dark glasses, a dark charcoal suit,
  white shirt, narrow dark tie, blue lanyard, and headset microphone. Facial hair is not visible;
  the lectern conceals build, so make no body-size claim. Keep the hair and glasses, but do not
  treat the lanyard, microphone, or lectern as fighter gear.

### Proposed specials

- **Rosetta Fold.** A loose chain folds into a rigid weapon. The hook is the
  [Rosetta protein-modeling and design suite](https://www.rosettacommons.org/docs/latest/getting-started/what-is-rosetta).
  Fictional caption: "I asked the amino acids nicely, and they formed a hammer."
- **De Novo Design.** A made-to-order protein becomes a shield, cage, or projectile. The hook is
  [de novo protein design](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-021-04184-w). Fictional caption:
  "Specification received: one extremely rude umbrella."
- **Top7.** A giant designed protein assembles as a trap. The hook is the original
  [Top7 design report](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.1089427). Fictional caption:
  "Evolution did not submit this blueprint, but the arena did."

## Thomas Steitz

- **Prize check:** Nobel Prize in Chemistry 2009, one third, "for studies of the structure and
  function of the ribosome." See the [Nobel Prize facts page](https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/chemistry/2009/steitz/facts/).
- **Proposed concept:** ribosome-scale molecular weapons factory.
- **Visual reference set:** [Yale's 2006 Zurich photo](https://tomsteitzsymposium.yale.edu/news/thomas-steitz-1940-2018-0)
  (credited there to Nenad Ban) and [2009 Nobel press-conference photo](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Nobel_Prize_2009-Press_Conference_KVA-10.jpg)
  provide separate 2006 and 2009 career periods. Both were downloaded to private scratch. The
  [Nobel biographical page](https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/chemistry/2009/steitz/biographical/)
  documents the Yale structural-biology career context.
- **Image-review trace:** selected period is the 2006-2009 late-career academic look. Both
  reviewed photos show white hair around a high receding forehead, thin wire-frame glasses, and a
  full short white beard. The 2009 photo adds a dark jacket, white shirt, and blue patterned tie;
  the 2006 photo shows a dark short-sleeved top. Neither supports a body-size claim.

### Proposed specials

- **50S Slam.** A large ribosomal subunit falls as a stage prop. The hook is the
  [50S subunit structure](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.289.5481.905). Fictional caption:
  "The large subunit has entered the chat at terminal velocity."
- **Peptidyl Transferase.** A growing peptide chain becomes a whip. The hook is the ribosome's
  [peptidyl-transferase center](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.289.5481.905). Fictional caption:
  "One more peptide bond, with feeling."
- **Translation Termination.** A ribosome reaches a cartoon stop codon and ejects its target. The
  hook is [translation termination](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK26829/). Fictional
  caption: "Stop codon found. Please exit the ribosome in an orderly fashion."

## Joachim Frank

- **Prize check:** Nobel Prize in Chemistry 2017, one third, "for developing cryo-electron
  microscopy for the high-resolution structure determination of biomolecules in solution." See
  the [Nobel Prize facts page](https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/chemistry/2017/frank/facts/).
- **Proposed concept:** freezes, images, averages, and reconstructs opponents.
- **Visual reference set:** [Joachim Frank Lab's earlier-published portrait](https://joachimfranklab.org/joachim-frank/)
  was published by December 2010 (its site upload path is `2010/12/team_joachim.jpg`), and the
  [2017 Nobel portrait](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Joachim_Frank_D81_4475_%2824027046267%29.jpg)
  records 2017-12-07 (Bengt Nyman). Both were downloaded to private scratch from distinct
  archives. The lab source establishes an earlier publication period but does not supply its
  original capture date; the 2017 source is the selected dated visual period.
- **Image-review trace:** the earlier-published portrait shows short light silver side-swept hair,
  a clean-shaven face, no visible glasses, a broad smile, and a dark small-check collared top; its
  crop has no build evidence. The selected 2017 Nobel look repeats the silver hair, clean-shaven
  face, and no visible glasses, adding a black formal jacket, white shirt, blue patterned tie, and
  small round gold lapel pin. The 2017 head-and-shoulders crop also has no build evidence.

### Proposed specials

- **Vitrification.** The opponent is held in stylized vitreous ice. The hook is
  [cryo-electron microscopy](https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/chemistry/2017/popular-information/).
  Fictional caption: "Please remain perfectly still while becoming glassy."
- **Particle Average.** Noisy copies align into a stronger image. The hook is Frank's method for
  [combining two-dimensional images into a three-dimensional reconstruction](https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/chemistry/2017/frank/facts/).
  Fictional caption: "After several hundred takes, that punch is finally in focus."
- **3D Reconstruction.** Projections orbit and assemble into a giant model. The hook is
  [single-particle reconstruction](https://doi.org/10.1016/0022-2836%2881%2990378-1). Fictional
  caption: "Your bad side has been computationally averaged away."

## Dorothy Crowfoot Hodgkin

- **Prize check:** Nobel Prize in Chemistry 1964, "for her determinations by X-ray techniques of
  the structures of important biochemical substances." See the [Nobel Prize facts page](https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/chemistry/1964/hodgkin/facts/).
- **Proposed concept:** crystals, diffraction, X-rays, and molecular structures.
- **Visual reference set:** [1970 chemistry-meeting photo at an atomic model](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:20._Tagung_1970_Chemiker%3B_Maik%C3%A4ferrede-_Dorothy_Hodgkin_am_Atommodell_-_LABW_-_Staatsarchiv_Freiburg_W_134_Nr._092470b.jpeg)
  (Staatsarchiv Freiburg ID W 134 Nr. 092470b) and [1983 chemistry-meeting photo](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:33._Tagung_1983_Chemiker-_Dorothy_Hodgkin_-_LABW_-_Staatsarchiv_Freiburg_W_134_Nr._120813b.jpeg)
  (Staatsarchiv Freiburg ID W 134 Nr. 120813b) provide dated later-career periods. Their Commons
  source records were downloaded to private scratch; repeated image-host HTTP 429 responses
  prevented direct inspection of those originals. [The Nobel biography](https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/chemistry/1964/hodgkin/biographical/)
  documents the Oxford research career.
- **Image-review trace:** selected period is the 1970 active-scientist period. The directly
  inspected black-and-white 1970 source shows pale/gray hair smoothly swept back from a side part
  to nape length, no visible glasses, and light-over-dark layered clothing. She leans forward over
  a table toward a large physical molecular model in a rectangular support frame. The dated 1983
  source, also directly inspected as a 700-pixel archive thumbnail, shows fuller soft/wavy white
  hair, no visible glasses, a dark open layer over a light collared blouse/dress, a broad draped
  stole, and a large oval pendant/brooch. Both images are torso crops; neither supports a body-size
  claim. Use the shared pale hair and light/dark layered clothing as the model base, and reserve
  the molecular-model pose or prop for a move/VFX. They are monochrome, so color and material are
  unspecified.

### Proposed specials

- **X-Ray Diffraction.** Crossing beams produce a spreading dot-pattern attack. The hook is
  [X-ray diffraction for molecular structure](https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/chemistry/1964/hodgkin/facts/).
  Fictional caption: "The pattern is beautiful. The damage report is less so."
- **Electron Density.** A glowing density map corrals the opponent. The hook is the
  [crystallographic structure determination of penicillin](https://doi.org/10.1038/162610a0).
  Fictional caption: "You are now constrained by a very persuasive contour map."
- **Vitamin B12.** A huge B12-shaped crystal descends over the arena. The hook is Hodgkin's
  [vitamin B12 structure determination](https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/chemistry/1964/hodgkin/facts/).
  Fictional caption: "A vitamin is technically not supposed to land this hard."

## Christian B. Anfinsen

- **Prize check:** Nobel Prize in Chemistry 1972, one half, "for his work on ribonuclease,
  especially concerning the connection between the amino acid sequence and the biologically active
  conformation." See the [Nobel Prize facts page](https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/chemistry/1972/anfinsen/facts/).
- **Proposed concept:** unfolding and spontaneous refolding.
- **Visual reference set:** [NIH's 1950s portrait on Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Christian_B._Anfinsen,_NIH_portrait,_1950s.jpg)
  and [1983 chemistry-meeting portrait](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:33._Tagung_1983_Chemiker_Christian_Anfinsen_-_W134Nr.120814c_-_Willy_Pragher.jpg)
  cover early NIH and later-career periods. Both were downloaded to private scratch. The
  [NIH biography](https://history.nih.gov/illustrated-histories/nih-biographies-plus/anfinsen-christian-boehmer-19161995)
  identifies his NIH career and 1962-1981 laboratory leadership.
- **Image-review trace:** selected period is the circa-1950s NIH portrait. Observed: dark smooth
  swept-back side-parted hair, clean-shaven face, medium-gray textured suit, bright white
  spread-collar shirt, and solid dark tie. Glasses and build are not visible; the chest-up,
  three-quarter portrait does not support a full-figure claim.

### Proposed specials

- **Denaturation.** The opponent becomes a floppy cartoon chain. The hook is Anfinsen's work on
  [ribonuclease folding](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.125.3249.114). Fictional caption:
  "A little unfolding never hurt anyone in a cartoon arena."
- **Sequence Determines Structure.** A displayed sequence folds into a weapon. The hook is the
  [sequence-to-active-conformation Nobel citation](https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/chemistry/1972/anfinsen/facts/).
  Fictional caption: "The instructions were in the sequence the whole time."
- **Anfinsen's Dogma.** A random chain disappears then reforms behind the opponent. The hook is
  [spontaneous refolding of ribonuclease](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.47.9.1309). Fictional
  caption: "I unfolded for dramatic effect and refolded behind you."

## Review notes

- The image evaluator inspected the downloaded sources. The selected cues above are limited to
  visible hair, glasses, facial-hair, and clothing features, rather than inferred identity traits.
- Frank and Steitz references are tight portraits. Hodgkin's dated 1970 and 1983 photos are
  monochrome torso crops. These limits are retained so later modeling does not invent
  biographical visual facts.
- All attack captions are fictional game writing. They do not claim that the scientists performed
  combat actions or that the science produces the depicted effects.
