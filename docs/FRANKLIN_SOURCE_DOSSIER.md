# Rosalind Franklin source dossier

## Scope

This dossier records the research inspiration for Franklin's three meter-selected specials. Franklin
was not a Nobel laureate; her roster entry accurately has no prize record. Each gameplay outcome is
fictional and uses the shared special scheduler.

## Source basis

- Franklin and Gosling's 1953 diffraction work reported the B form of DNA and its helical
  structure. [Franklin and Gosling, _Nature_ (1953)](https://doi.org/10.1038/171740a0)
- The Rosalind Franklin Institute summarizes Photo 51 as an X-ray diffraction image central to
  understanding DNA structure. [Photo 51 history](https://www.rfi.ac.uk/about-us/rosalind-franklin/)

## Research-motif specials

- **Photo 51.** Real work: X-ray diffraction produced the characteristic DNA image. [Franklin and
  Gosling (1953)](https://doi.org/10.1038/171740a0). Game fiction: intersecting, guard-piercing
  X-pattern projectiles.
- **Crystal Fiber.** Real work: oriented DNA fibers made diffraction measurements possible.
  [Franklin and Gosling (1953)](https://doi.org/10.1038/171740a0). Game fiction: a brief nearby
  stun.
- **Double Helix.** Real work: the B-form diffraction pattern supported a helical DNA structure.
  [Franklin and Gosling (1953)](https://doi.org/10.1038/171740a0). Game fiction: a zone followed
  by a strike.

## Evidence limits

The sources establish diffraction research and its structural conclusion. They do not establish
combat techniques, damage, timing, or physical effects; those remain game data.
