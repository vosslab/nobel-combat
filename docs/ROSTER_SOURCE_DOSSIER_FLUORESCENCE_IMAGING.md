- Nobel citation: "for the discovery and development of the green fluorescent protein, GFP."
- Chooser source:
  [Roger Y. Tsien Nobel facts](https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/chemistry/2008/tsien/facts/).

## Dennis Gabor

### Prize record

- Nobel Prize in Physics, 1971.
- Nobel citation: "for his invention and development of the holographic method."
- Chooser source:
  [Dennis Gabor Nobel facts](https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/physics/1971/gabor/facts/).

### Likeness record

The references cover the late active Imperial College period and the 1971 prize period. Gabor
joined Imperial in 1949 and remained through his 1967 retirement, as documented in his
[Nobel biographical page](https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/physics/1971/gabor/biographical/).
Choose the 1967 appearance: it is an active-career photograph rather than an automatic
Nobel-ceremony choice.

### Special research and captions

#### Interference Pattern

- Real hook: Gabor's [Nobel Lecture](https://www.nobelprize.org/uploads/2018/06/gabor-lecture.pdf)
  describes holography as recording interference and reconstructing wavefronts.
- Fictional caption: "Congratulations: your wavefront has developed an argument with mine."

#### Holographic Double

- Real hook: [The 1971 Nobel Prize summary](https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/physics/1971/summary/)
  identifies Gabor's invention and development of holography.
- Fictional caption: "This duplicate is three-dimensional and somehow better at blocking."

#### Wavefront Reconstruction

- Real hook: Gabor's [Nobel Lecture](https://www.nobelprize.org/uploads/2018/06/gabor-lecture.pdf)
  covers the reconstruction of a recorded wavefront with a reference beam.
- Fictional caption: "The smudge was the whole plan. Please meet the reconstructed department."
